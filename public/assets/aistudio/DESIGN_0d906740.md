# Design System: Dark Red Editorial (Risna Shofiyatuz Zahro)

## Brand Personality & Aesthetic
- **Style**: Dark, cinematic, editorial, bold, and high-precision tech aesthetic.
- **Tone**: Calm, premium, confident, academic yet cutting-edge informatics & AI enthusiasm.
- **Concept**: Near-black void with high-contrast crimson/ruby red accents, monumental condensed typography, hairline dividers, and minimal outlined containers.

---

## Color Palette

### Base & Backgrounds
- `--bg`: `#070707` (Deep Obsidian / Canvas Primary)
- `--bg-2`: `#0E0E0E` (Elevated Surface / Card Background)
- `--panel`: `#111111` (Component & Modal Surface)
- `--panel-card`: `#141414` (Hover / Interactive Surface)

### Typography & Neutrals
- `--text-primary`: `#F5F5F5` (High-contrast pure white)
- `--text-secondary`: `#C4C4C4` (Neutral light grey)
- `--muted`: `#9A9A9A` (Subtle metadata & label text)
- `--line`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` (Hairline borders & separators)
- `--line-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` (Grid & nested dividers)

### Red Accents (Signature Theme)
- `--red`: `#D1121B` (Primary brand accent, giant display gradients, large numbers, active borders)
- `--red-text`: `#F0434B` (Accessible high-contrast red for small text, badges, and icon fills)
- `--red-line`: `rgba(209, 18, 27, 0.38)` (Accented divider line & highlight glow)
- `--red-deep`: `#3B0508` (Shadows, gradient stops, and deep atmospheric glows)
- `--panel-red`: `linear-gradient(160deg, #A30F16 0%, #5E080D 55%, #2A0407 100%)` (Signature quote and highlight panel gradient)

---

## Typography

### Font Families
- **Display & Large Numerals**: `'Anton', 'Impact', sans-serif`
  - Style: Uppercase, tight line-height (0.85 - 0.95), letter-spacing `0.005em` to `0.02em`.
  - Usage: Giant wordmarks ("PORTFOLIO"), main title ("RISNA SHOFIYATUZ ZAHRO"), section counter numerals ("01", "02", "90.00").
- **Body, UI & Technical**: `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`
  - Weights: `300` (Light quotes), `400` (Regular body), `500` (Medium UI), `600` (Semi-bold labels), `700` (Bold section titles).
  - Usage: Paragraphs, descriptions, badges, technical details, navigation.
- **Accents & Signatures**: `'Allura', 'Mrs Saint Delafield', cursive`
  - Style: Elegant italic script, natural handwriting flourish.
  - Usage: "Hello, I'm" hero lead-in, personal signature block.

### Type Scale
- `Display XXL`: `clamp(64px, 10vw, 140px)` / Line-height `0.85` (Hero name & Giant backdrop)
- `Display XL`: `46px - 56px` / Line-height `0.9` (Big stat numbers, modal titles)
- `Heading 1`: `22px - 28px` / Line-height `1.2` / Weight `700` / Uppercase (Section headers)
- `Heading 2`: `16px - 18px` / Line-height `1.3` / Weight `600` (Card titles, experience roles)
- `Body`: `13.5px - 15px` / Line-height `1.65` / Weight `400` (Narrative text, descriptions)
- `Technical Label / Badge`: `10.5px - 11.5px` / Line-height `1.0` / Weight `600` / Letter-spacing `0.14em` / Uppercase

---

## Components & Patterns

### 1. Section Header (`.sec-head`)
- Horizontal flex row aligning section title (Inter 700, uppercase), expanding 1px hairline divider (`--line`), and right-aligned interactive link (`.label` with arrow indicator `→`).

### 2. Outlined Chips & Tags (`.chip`)
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`
- Radius: `4px`
- Padding: `8px 14px`
- Font: Inter 600, 10.5px, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.12em`
- Interactive Hover: Border changes to `--red`, text turns `--red-text`, subtle `translateY(-2px)`.

### 3. Experience & Project Cards (`.xcard`)
- Aspect ratio thumbnail header with 3-dot simulated terminal/browser bar (`rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`).
- Huge hollow/outlined number counter, crisp title, role metadata, and directional arrow.
- Interaction: Smooth image zoom `scale(1.04)`, border color transition to `--red`, and red accent glow.

### 4. Interactive Timeline Step
- Grid structure: `[Number | 44px Circular Node | Content Block]`.
- Circular node in `#0B0B0B` with `1px solid --red` and glowing inner SVG icon (`--red-text`).
- Vertical connector line with dynamic draw animation (`--red-line`).

### 5. Signature Accent Panel
- Dark crimson gradient container (`--panel-red`) with ambient grain overlay, oversized quotation mark glyph (`❝`), serif/script autograph, and call-to-action badge.

---

## Spacing, Borders & Effects
- **Outer Padding**: `clamp(20px, 4vw, 56px)`
- **Divider Rule**: `1px solid var(--line)` (hairline precision)
- **Border Radius**: `4px` (Chips & Cards), `6px` (Panels & Modals), `9999px` (Status pills & Avatars)
- **Focus States**: `2px solid var(--red-text)` with `3px` offset
- **Vignette & Glow**: Soft radial gradients with `rgba(209, 18, 27, 0.25)` to create depth without compromising readability.
